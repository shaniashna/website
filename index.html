The Personal Mission

"Treat others as you would like to be treated."

I founded Ashna Consulting on this simple motto. My journey from Iran to Ukraine to Berlin taught me that bureaucracy isn't just paperwork—it's personal. I didn't just learn the system from a textbook; I lived it, and I understand the anxiety behind every official letter.

Beyond consulting, I work in the high-pressure environment of healthcare administration. I handle your case with the same organization, data privacy standards, and clarity that I apply to sensitive patient care.

My goal is to be the bridge between you and the authorities. Whether in English, German, or Farsi, I don't just translate words—I translate the system, ensuring you never feel lost.
